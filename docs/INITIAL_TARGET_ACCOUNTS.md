**Purpose:** Prospecting hypotheses for customer discovery, not evidence of buying intent.  
**Important:** No company below is claimed to have a security problem or to be a customer. Verify the current product and identify the right role before outreach. Start with interviews, not a hard sell.

## Prioritization method

- **P1 — discovery first:** agent product with customer-facing or business-action workflows; potentially reachable team.
- **P2 — later / partner:** platform, framework, or established vendor; useful for ecosystem learning, integrations, or later channel discussions.
- **P3 — aspirational:** larger incumbent; not a first-customer assumption.

## Outreach sequence

1. Verify that the product currently supports actions/tools relevant to HyperGen's initial use case.
2. Find a public company channel or professional role; do not guess email addresses.
3. Personalize one sentence based on a public source.
4. Ask how they test tool actions, authorization, privacy, and state changes today.
5. Request a 15-minute discovery conversation.
6. Only after a clear problem emerges, offer a narrowly scoped synthetic or authorized pilot.
7. Track responses, objections, current tools, budget ownership, and next steps.

## Message template

Hi [Name] — I saw [specific public product/workflow]. I'm building HyperGen, an early-stage harness that tests tool-using AI agents in controlled scenarios and verifies the resulting state against expected policy outcomes.

I'm trying to learn how teams currently test things like unauthorized tool actions, prompt injection in customer data, and false claims that an action succeeded. How do you handle that today? Would you be open to a short discovery chat?

We're early and looking for feedback; I'm not assuming your product has these problems.

## Research notes and caveats

- Relevance AI's product page describes evaluations, pre-publish eval sets, tool access, and governance. This suggests an informed buyer or competitor benchmark, not an easy sale.
- Gumloop's public product describes agents, tool connections, browser sessions, and audit logs. Ask about existing safeguards before pitching.
- LangChain, CrewAI, n8n, Composio, and Browserbase may be ecosystem/adapter partners as much as direct buyers.
- The best first buyer may be a smaller company not listed here. Continue to prioritize actual pain, access to the decision-maker, and willingness to pilot over brand recognition.
